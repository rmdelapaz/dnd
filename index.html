The Dungeons & Dragons 5e Guide

Dungeons & Dragons from Wizards of the Coast is the world's best-known tabletop roleplaying game: a group of friends, a Dungeon Master, and a d20 that decides what happens next. This 10-part guide follows the 5th Edition rules (the 2014 Player's Handbook) and takes you from your first character to running your own table, or playing on your own.

D&D 5e at a Glance

Four ideas carry most of the game. Each part of the guide builds on them.

One Die to Rule Them

Roll a d20, add a modifier, and meet or beat a Difficulty Class (DC) or Armor Class (AC). Easy is DC 10, medium 15, hard 20.

Advantage

Good circumstances give advantage: roll two d20s and keep the higher. Bad ones give disadvantage: keep the lower. They never stack.

Proficiency

Things your character is trained in add a proficiency bonus that grows from +2 at 1st level to +6 at 17th.

Your Turn

Move, take one action, and maybe a bonus action, plus one reaction each round, like an opportunity attack.
